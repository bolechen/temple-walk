High-resolution images and data related to the CMA’s collection are made available under a Creative Commons Zero (opens in a new tab) (CC0) designation. What is open access? Open access content (images, data, software, and documentation) is content that the CMA publishes digitally, free of charge, and without copyright and licensing restrictions. For more information about open access, visit the Creative Commons (opens in a new tab) website for an overview.
